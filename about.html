01 // Who We Are

More Than
A Contract.

Canvas Sports Agency is a full-service sports representation firm built on the belief that athletes deserve more than just a contract—they deserve a career roadmap, personal guidance, and a team that fights for them. Founded by Rashaad Ubah, an NBPA and FIBA-certified agent, we are dedicated to ensuring young athletes have the knowledge, tools, and protection to succeed.

Unlike large agencies that focus on a few superstars, we work closely with every athlete we sign, treating them as family, not statistics. Rashaad's personal journey as a former athlete who navigated the complexities of sports business after an eligibility-altering NIL experience shaped his mission to protect and elevate young athletes so they never feel lost, exploited, or unprepared.

02 // Mission Statement

Protecting
Futures.

Empowering Athletes, Protecting Futures & Maximizing Potential. At Canvas Sports Agency, we don’t just represent athletes—we develop legacies. We believe every athlete is more than just their stats; they are brands, entrepreneurs, and future leaders.

Our mission is to provide personalized mentorship, strategic NIL opportunities, and long-term career planning that empower high school and college athletes to thrive both on and off the field. Canvas stands as a shield and a guide. We provide boutique, athlete-first representation, ensuring that our clients receive the attention, education, and protection they need.

At Canvas, every athlete is a priority, not a number. Your future, your brand, your success—we’re with you every step of the way.
